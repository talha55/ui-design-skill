# Wildfold: direction summary

The client said "just build it", so the checkpoint was skipped and this summary is the record of what was decided on their behalf.

**The idea.** Wildfold is a garden you walk into: a hero that opens like a garden door onto a living flower, then a year of seasons that turn as you scroll. Signature: bold editorial (type is the main visual, paper and ink plus one strong colour in blocks, masks, wipes, a background that changes as sections arrive), with one cinematic pinned scene for the seasons.

**Style.** Loose and warm, like a cut-flower bucket on a cottage step: leaf green and paper cream, one hot lily orange used only for the buttons you should press.

**Palette.**
- Paper `#F4EFE4`: page background, text on dark sections.
- Paper deep `#E8E0CE`: marquee band, quiet surfaces.
- Ink `#11261A`: body text, hero and seasons backdrop, footer.
- Leaf `#1E4A2C` (dominant): large colour blocks (manifesto, subscriptions).
- Muted `#55614F`: secondary text on paper (5.5:1 on paper).
- Line `#CFC6B0`: one-pixel borders.
- Lily `#FF6B3D`: primary buttons only. Text on it is ink (5.5:1); white would fail.

**Type.** Fraunces (Google Fonts, variable, soft and wonky axes on) for headings: a soft, slightly hand-grown serif with a lovely italic, set very large. Hanken Grotesk (Google Fonts) for text: quiet and readable. Weights used: 400, 500, 600.

**Sections.**
1. Hero: full-viewport looping video of a garden lily, headline, two actions (wedding enquiry first, subscription second), phone in the header.
2. Manifesto: what Wildfold is, in the studio's own plain words, on a leaf-green block; words fill in as you scroll.
3. Seasons: pinned scene, four seasons replace each other over full-screen photographs.
4. Weddings and events: the main goal. Image wipe, short copy, enquiry form.
5. Marquee band: the studio's own words running across the page.
6. Weekly subscriptions: the second goal. Leaf-green block, arch photo with parallax, sign-up form.
7. Saturday workshop: horizontal gallery driven by vertical scroll (swipeable row on phones).
8. Visit: address, days, phone, email.
9. Ending: very large closing line, final actions, and a giant "Wildfold" wordmark filled with a photograph.

**Photos.** Loose, garden-grown flowers in natural light: hand-tied wild bunches, dahlias, anemones, event tables, hands arranging. No staged smiling people, no visible brand names, no faces. Stock stand-ins until the studio supplies its own.

**Moments.**
- Opening: the video opens from an arch (a garden door) to full screen while the headline rises line by line.
- Scroll story: the manifesto fills in word by word; images wipe open; the seasons pinned scene is the centrepiece.
- Horizontal workshop gallery driven by vertical scroll.
- Pointer: buttons lean toward the cursor, a soft cursor ring grows over links.
- Ending: a giant "Wildfold" rises from behind a mask, filled with a dahlia photograph that drifts as you scroll.

**Motion.** Full.

**Left out on purpose.** Reviews, ratings, prices, plan names, opening times, workshop length or cost, team names, years in business, a logo (a typeset wordmark stands in), a map. None were supplied. Visible dashed placeholders mark the gaps for prices and opening times.
