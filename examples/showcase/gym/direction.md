# Northline Strength: direction summary

The checkpoint was skipped because the client said "Just build it". This is the direction the page was built to.

**The idea:** A gym with no mirrors and no machines has nothing to look at but the iron, so the page is type and iron and almost nothing else. One vertical line, the north line, runs down the whole page and fills as you scroll; the bar is loaded plate by plate until the room is full at eight. Signature: bold editorial (type is the picture, strong grid broken on purpose), with a dark iron base and one chalk-paper chapter.

**Style:** Blunt and industrial, like a foundry floor at 6 am: near-black iron, chalk white, huge condensed capitals, and one molten orange used only where you act.

**Palette**
- Iron `#0E1113`: page background, headings on chalk, text on the orange
- Iron raised `#161A1D`: inputs and hover surfaces
- Plate `#22282C`: barbell plates in the centrepiece
- Rule `#2C3338`: one-pixel borders on iron
- Chalk `#ECE9E1`: text on iron, and the paper chapter ("the floor")
- Chalk rule `#CBC7BB`: borders on chalk
- Muted on iron `#A3A8A6` (about 7:1) and muted on chalk `#4F5355` (about 6:1): secondary text
- Molten `#FF5A1F`: primary buttons, the north line, the strike-throughs, the eighth plate, and the closing booking block. Text on it is iron (about 6:1). It is never used for text on chalk (fails 3:1).

**Type:** Big Shoulders Display (Google Fonts, 700 and 900) for headings and numbers; Instrument Sans (Google Fonts, 400 and 600) for text and labels. Neither is a default generated-page face.

**Sections**
1. Hero: "No mirrors. No machines. Just iron." over a looping chalk-and-grip video, with the free-session button and the address and phone on the first screen.
2. Kit band: a slanted chalk strip running "Barbells, Kettlebells, Sleds, Rigs, No machines, No mirrors" across the seam.
3. Centrepiece: a pinned scene where eight plates load onto a bar while a giant numeral counts up to 8 and the captions change: "Coached. Small groups. Eight at most."
4. Three ways to train: small-group classes, open gym, one-to-one coaching, as big rows with wiped-open photos.
5. The floor (chalk chapter): what is on the floor, what is not (machines and mirrors struck through), with an overlapping photo collage and a plain note that the photos are stand-ins.
6. Class times: a horizontal run of huge times (weekdays 6, 7, noon, 5:30, 6:30; Saturday 8, 9) driven by vertical scroll, each with a "Book this time" button that pre-fills the form. A swipeable row on phones.
7. Book and visit: "First session is free." on the orange block, the form, address, phone, email, and a closing oversized NORTHLINE wordmark.

**Photos:** Black-and-white treatment, tight crops, chalk, iron plates, kettlebells, bare racks, hard light. Avoid faces, mirrors, machines, branded kit, smiling models. Stock stands in for the gym and is labelled as such.

**Moments**
- The opening: the headline rises line by line over a slow-settling video while the north line draws in.
- The centrepiece: the bar loads to eight as you scroll.
- The strike: "Machines" and "Mirrors" are crossed out in orange as they arrive, on the chalk chapter.
- The week: a horizontal run of giant class times.
- The ending: the page turns fully orange, "First session is free.", and the NORTHLINE wordmark rises from the footer.

**Motion:** Full. Lenis smooth scroll, Motion library, magnetic primary buttons, press feedback, link underlines that draw in. Reduced motion gives a calm, complete static page (no pinning, no autoplay, no smooth scroll).

**Left out on purpose:** reviews, ratings, coach names and bios, prices, years in business, member counts, logo (a plain text wordmark stands in), brand colours (chosen here), a map, open gym hours, class length, and any claim about what the free first session includes.
