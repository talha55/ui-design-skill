# Tidewell Plumbing and Gas: direction summary

The checkpoint was skipped because the client said "Just build it". This is what was decided on their behalf.

- **The idea:** The name Tidewell is filled with water on load, and a rising tide is the thread through the whole page: a visitor opens it and the business's own name fills up. Signature: bold editorial (paper and ink plus one strong colour in blocks, huge type, hard crops), with one cinematic pinned scene as the centrepiece.
- **Style:** Blunt and loud, like a plant room at 2 am: deep teal-black, sea-glass teal blocks, and one flame-orange button so the phone number is never in doubt.
- **Palette:**
  - Ink `#08262A`: hero, scenes, footer, text on paper.
  - Ink 2 `#0E3A3F`: one step up from ink, for surfaces and the footer water.
  - Tide `#3CC0B4`: water, the facts block, small marks.
  - Paper `#EEEADF`: page background and text on ink.
  - Paper 2 `#E2DDCF`: quote section surface.
  - Muted `#46605F` (on paper) and `#9DB8B5` (on ink): secondary text.
  - Accent `#FF5A1F`: call and quote buttons only. Text on it is ink (5.1:1).
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display 900 (headings, Google Fonts), a tall industrial face that can fill the screen; Schibsted Grotesk 400 and 700 (text, Google Fonts).
- **Sections:**
  1. Hero: the water-filled name, what and where, call button.
  2. Services marquee band: the six services as a running line.
  3. Scene (pinned): six things people say before they ring, while the water rises; ends on the call button.
  4. Services: six big rows, a photo follows the cursor, tapping a row starts the quote.
  5. Facts block: owner-operated, two vans, homes and small businesses (only what was supplied).
  6. Emergency band: "Any hour. Ring." over a parallax photo.
  7. Area and hours: Brackenford, a Monday to Saturday strip with Sunday as emergency only.
  8. Quote: short form that opens an email, plus the number again.
  9. Ending: the phone number at 15 percent of the viewport width with water rising behind it.
- **Photos:** Dark, wet, metallic scenes: pipes, taps, flame, tools, copper. No faces, no signage. Stock stand-ins, never presented as Tidewell's work.
- **Moments:**
  1. The opening: the name fills with water that sloshes towards the pointer.
  2. The centrepiece: a framed photo opens to full screen, then the water climbs through six jobs and floods the page to the colour of the next section.
  3. Services: a photo follows the cursor from row to row.
  4. The ending: the phone number with water rising behind it.
  Also: a tide staff down the right edge that fills as you scroll.
- **Motion:** Full. Reduced motion gives a calm, complete page (the scene becomes a normal stacked section).
- **Left out on purpose:** reviews, ratings, years in business, licence numbers, prices, guarantees, team photos, street address, suburb names, social links, a map, a logo. None were supplied.
