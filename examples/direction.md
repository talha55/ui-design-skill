# Direction: Tidewell Plumbing and Gas

The client said "just build it", so the checkpoint was skipped and this summary records what was decided on their behalf.

- **Style:** Plain-spoken and sturdy, like the lettering on a work van: deep harbour blue-green, big type with plenty of open space, and copper-amber used only on the call and quote buttons.
- **Palette:**
  - Page background `#F2F6F5`: main page background, a cool off-white.
  - Surface `#FFFFFF`: the quote form card.
  - Text `#0B2227`: body text and footer background.
  - Muted text `#4B6469`: secondary text and form field borders.
  - Hairline `#D2DEDC`: dividers between rows.
  - Dominant `#0C3B42`: header, hero, contact band, headings.
  - Dominant tint `#E1EEEC`: the owner-operated section.
  - Text on dominant `#B9D3D0`: secondary text on dark areas.
  - Accent `#F0A03C`: call and quote buttons only (dark text `#0B2227` on it, 7.7:1).
  - Error `#A32A1F`: form error messages.
- **Type:** Archivo Bold for headings, Public Sans (regular, semibold, bold) for text. Both from Google Fonts.
- **Sections:**
  1. Header: wordmark and a tappable phone number, always visible.
  2. Hero: what Tidewell does, where, and the call button, with a quieter "Request a quote".
  3. Facts strip: days open, emergency call-outs, area.
  4. Services: numbered list of five, with emergency call-outs set apart as its own call panel.
  5. Owner-operated: what the brief says about the business (owner, two vans, homes and small commercial).
  6. Area and hours: service area and days, with labelled gaps for suburbs and times.
  7. Quote: phone, email and a short form that opens the visitor's email app.
  8. Footer: contact details and a licence placeholder.
- **Photos:** Real working scenes, hands and pipework, cool daylight with blue tones that sit with the palette. No faces, no uniforms, no handshakes, no visible brands or van signage. Stock only, labelled as stock in the footer.
- **Motion:** Medium. Hover and press feedback on buttons and links; each section's content fades and rises in once as it scrolls into view. The hero and call buttons are visible on first paint. Nothing moves when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Left out on purpose:** reviews, ratings, years in business, licence numbers, prices, guarantees, "free quote", response times, suburb names, social links, a map and a street address. None were supplied.
