What we do

Six jobs, done properly.

From a slow drain to a full bathroom, one local team handles both the plumbing and the gas.

  • Blocked drains

    Slow sinks, backed-up showers and blocked toilets cleared, with the cause found so it does not keep coming back.

  • Hot water systems

    Repairs, replacements and new installs for gas, electric and solar-boosted systems. No cold showers while you wait.

  • Gas fitting

    Gas appliance installs, new connections, servicing and safety checks, all handled by a qualified gas fitter.

  • Leak detection

    Hidden leaks, damp walls and mystery water bills traced to the source before they turn into bigger repairs.

  • Bathroom renovations

    Plumbing-led bathroom refits, from moving a vanity to a full strip-out and rebuild, managed from start to finish.

  • Emergency call-outs

    Burst pipe, gas smell, no hot water, overflowing drain. Call any time of day or night and we will get a van moving.
