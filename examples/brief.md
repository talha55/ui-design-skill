Build a one-page website for Tidewell Plumbing and Gas, a small plumbing and gas-fitting
business in the town of Brackenford. (The business and the town are invented for this example.)

- Owner-operated, two vans, residential and small commercial work.
- Services: blocked drains, hot water systems, gas fitting, leak detection, bathroom renovations, emergency call-outs.
- Service area: Brackenford and nearby suburbs.
- Phone: 0400 000 000. Email: hello@tidewell.example.
- Open Monday to Saturday, emergency call-outs at any time.
- Goal of the page: get people to call or request a quote.
- No logo, brand colours, photos, reviews or licence numbers have been supplied.
